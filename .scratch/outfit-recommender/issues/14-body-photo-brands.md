# 14: Body-photo Outfits: Brands become Pieces

**What to build:** Full-Body and Body-Only Consultations get complete Outfits. For each Style Icon, Qloo picks the Brands its fans favour, our rules turn them into a Top, a Bottom and Shoes, and the AI tailors each into a concrete Piece. Each row shows the item, its Brand, why it suits, the Brand's price range and a "Visit <Brand>" link, and each card shows the Outfit's total price range. Budget Tiers come in ticket 15; until then every body-photo Consultation is at No Budget.

**Blocked by:** [07 Photo check rejections and the one-tap Photo Type switch](07-photo-check-rejections.md), [11 Hairstyle Pieces tailored to the photo](11-hairstyle-pieces.md)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Spec:** [spec.md](../spec.md) sections "Outfit assembly (pure module)" (Brands, the Style Preference rule, Usable Brands, Pieces, De-duplication, Categories by photo, Price range, "Visit <Brand>", Config, denylist seeds), "Stylings" (How many Outfits; Tailoring failures), "Qloo client and cache" (Rate limits) and "AI provider interface" (2. Tailor: In, Rules). Stories 83, 92 (the price range), 93, 96 and 109. [ADR-0001](../../../docs/adr/0001-qloo-selects-ai-concretizes.md), [ADR-0002](../../../docs/adr/0002-photo-style-steers-style-icons.md).

- [x] Body-photo Stylings gain a Brands stage: one merged Brand call per Style Icon, built by the assembly module with `filter.type=urn:entity:brand`; `signal.interests.entities=<Style Icon>`; `signal.demographics.age`, and `signal.demographics.gender` from the Outfit's line; `signal.location.query=<Country>`; `operator.filter.tags=intersection`, with `filter.tags` = `genre:brand:fashion`, the age group's `target_age_group` tag and `key_market:qloo:<Country>` when Qloo has one for that Country; `filter.popularity.min=0.98`; and `take=50`. The Photo Style is never sent on Brand calls.
- [x] Queries use the Style Preference, Country and age group stored on the Consultation. A no-preference user's Brand calls and tailoring use the line of the Outfit's Style Icon.
- [x] Each Country in config gains its `key_market` tag where Qloo has one.
- [x] The first Styling fetches Brands for the first 3 Style Icons in parallel and later ones as needed, for at most 6 Style Icons per Styling, with at most 3 Qloo calls in flight at once.
- [x] The Style Preference rule, applied to `target_gender`: menswear keeps a Brand that includes Male or doesn't include Female; womenswear keeps a Brand that includes Female or doesn't include Male.
- [x] Usable Brands are only those tagged apparel, or footwear/shoes/sneakers, which drops watches, jewellery, eyewear and lingerie. Then the retailer and off-category denylists apply. Both live in config, match by entity ID with the name kept for readability, and are seeded from the spec.
- [x] Pieces are picked in this order: Shoes, the highest-affinity unused footwear Brand; Top, the highest-affinity unused apparel Brand; Bottom, the next apparel Brand, preferring a denim Brand in the raw top 10, which may be the Top's Brand. Shoes never share a Brand with the Top or Bottom of the same Outfit. A Style Icon that can't complete an Outfit is skipped.
- [x] No Brand repeats within a Category across the Outfits a Styling builds. Each Outfit stores its Brand picks separately from its Pieces.
- [x] Categories by photo: a Full-Body Photo gets Hairstyle, Top, Bottom and Shoes, with no Hairstyle when the hair is covered; a Body-Only Photo gets Top, Bottom and Shoes.
- [x] Tailoring now also receives each Brand Piece's Brand, Qloo category tags and price range. Its rules: items are generic, never product or model names; never a price; only an item the Brand plausibly makes; the Top is one garment, the main visible upper layer. The fake adapter builds generic Top, Bottom and Shoes items.
- [x] A substitute after failed tailoring fetches Brands for the next Style Icon when it needs to.
- [x] Brand Pieces store the Brand's entity ID, name, official site and price minimum and maximum.
- [x] Each Brand row shows its Category, the item, its Brand, why it suits, its price range (`price_range_usd_min`–`max`) and a "Visit <Brand>" link to the Brand's `official_site` when that's an https URL; otherwise the row has no link. The card shows the Outfit's range: the sums of its Brand Pieces' minimums and maximums, leaving out any Piece with no price data.
- [x] Pure tests cover the Brand query for each body Photo Type, age group, Country with and without a `key_market` tag, and Style Preference; the Style Preference rule; the apparel and footwear filter; both denylists; the pick order, the denim preference and Shoes never sharing a Brand with the Top or Bottom; de-duplication across a Styling's Outfits; skipping a Style Icon that can't complete an Outfit; price-range sums; and the `official_site` link rule.
- [x] A server-operation test covers covered hair dropping the Hairstyle from a Full-Body Photo's Outfits.

## Comments

Implemented. Decisions made along the way that later tickets should know:

- **The module.** `src/server/outfit-assembly/brands.ts` holds `needsBrands`, `brandQuery`, `usableBrands`, `pickBrands`, `planOutfitBrands`, `brandsUsedBy`, `brandPriceRange`, `outfitPriceRange` and `visitUrl`, with pure tests in `brands.test.ts`. The age, line-gender and Country signals both queries send moved into `signals.ts`, and `PriceRangeUsd` and `BRAND_CATEGORIES` live in `src/config/consultations.ts`.
  - The Brand query takes the Consultation's age group and Country and the Style Icon's entity ID and line. It has no Budget Tier yet: ticket 15 adds the tier's tags, the price guard and the widening list to `brandQuery`. The age groups' `target_age_group` tags are `BRAND_TARGET_AGE_GROUPS` in `src/config/qloo.ts`, without the widen-to column, which ticket 15 adds.
  - Brands are ranked by `query.affinity`, stably, with any lacking one last, as Style Icons are. "Raw top 10" for the denim preference is Qloo's own order, before any filter, so a denim Brand pushed down by dropped watches or retailers doesn't count.
  - The Bottom is the highest-affinity apparel Brand not yet a Bottom, other than the Shoes' and the Top's, preferring a denim Brand in the raw top 10 even when it's the Top's. With no other apparel Brand left, it is the Top's Brand.
  - Denim is `product_category:qloo:denim` or `genre:brand:fashion:jeans`. Footwear is `footwear`, `shoes` or `sneakers`. Apparel is `apparel`.
  - The Style Preference rule compares `target_gender` case-insensitively.
  - `visitUrl` returns the parsed URL's `href`, so `https://x.example` becomes `https://x.example/`.
  - The Shoes are picked first, as the spec orders it, so a Brand making both clothes and Shoes can leave a Style Icon with no apparel Brand, and it is skipped even though another footwear Brand would have let it complete.
- **Countries.** Each Country has `qlooKeyMarket`, the full tag ID or null. 213 of the 230 have one; the 17 without are small territories (Falklands, Guernsey, Pitcairn…). Found on 2026-10-17 with read-only tag lookups (`filter.type=urn:tag`, `filter.tag.types=urn:tag:key_market:qloo`, `filter.parents.types=urn:entity:brand`, `filter.results.tags=` the candidates), each Country under its own name, its Qloo location and common other names. Where Qloo has several, the most popular is kept: `united_states` over `usa`, `united_kingdom` over `england`, `ivory_coast` over `cote_d_ivoire`, `south_korea` over `korea`.
  - **For ticket 15:** some tags are on very few Brands (Saint Kitts and Nevis, Nauru, Tuvalu, Vatican City sit at popularity 0.2–0.6), and the research found India and Nigeria thin too. With the intersection operator, those Consultations get few or no usable Brands until widening drops the `key_market` tag.
- **The denylists** (`RETAILER_DENYLIST`, `OFF_CATEGORY_DENYLIST` in `src/config/denylists.ts`) hold the spec's seeds, with IDs from read-only `/search` calls on 2026-10-17. Qloo spells two differently: "Net a porter" and "My Theresa". Muse by Berta, Berta's bridal line, is in too, since it carries an apparel tag. For the team to decide, not added: Alex and Ani (jewellery with an apparel tag, named in the research) and Lojas Riachuelo (a Brazilian department store, which has no category tag, so it drops anyway).
- **The steps.** `planOutfits(stylingId, brandsByStyleIcon)` now returns `{ fetchBrands, tries }`, and there's a new Qloo step, `fetchBrands(stylingId, styleIconId)`, with `QLOO_STEP_MAX_RETRIES`.
  - `buildOutfits` keeps the fetched Brands by Style Icon ID in the run and hands them to every `planOutfits`. Like the people in ticket 10, they're step data within the run, and the Qloo cache keeps them across runs, so a Try again (ticket 29) refetches through the cache.
  - `planOutfits` plans Style Icons in order. It picks Brands only from Style Icons whose Brands have come back, and stops at the first that hasn't. A Style Icon that can't complete an Outfit beside the Outfits already built is marked skipped there, without counting as a tailoring failure. One held back only by Outfits planned in the same round is left unused instead, since their Brands are freed if their tailoring fails. When it still needs Style Icons, it asks for the next ones' Brands instead of tailoring: 3 the first time in a first Styling (`BRAND_FETCHES_UP_FRONT`), otherwise as many as it still needs, never more than `QLOO_CALLS_AT_ONCE` (3) at once or `MAX_BRAND_FETCHES_PER_STYLING` (6) in a Styling. All are in `src/config/stylings.ts`.
  - Picks are fixed before tailoring and travel in the `OutfitTry` to `tailorOutfit`, which saves them on the Outfit. Picks for a Style Icon whose tailoring failed are free again for its substitute.
  - De-duplication counts only the Styling's own Outfits (`outfit.styling_id`). **For ticket 26:** "Show 2 more Outfits" must also count the current Outfits' picks, and plan from the Consultation's current Style Icons.
  - The stage is `brands` after `saveStyleIcons` for a body photo, and moves to `building_outfits` with the first tailoring. Later fetches, for substitutes or skipped Style Icons, don't move it back.
  - `planPreviews` no longer checks for Outfits with no Pieces: every Outfit has one now. Its test, which used a Body-Only Photo, is gone.
- **Data.** Migration 0006 adds `outfit.top_brand_entity_id`, `bottom_brand_entity_id` and `shoes_brand_entity_id` (all set or all null), and `piece.brand_entity_id`, `brand_name`, `brand_official_site`, `brand_price_usd_min` and `brand_price_usd_max` (doubles). A CHECK makes a Piece's Brand set exactly when it isn't the Hairstyle. **For ticket 16:** that CHECK already covers a One-Piece.
- **Tailoring.** `TailoringInput.pieces` is now `TailoringPiece[]`: the Hairstyle alone, or a Brand Piece with `brand: { name, categoryTags, priceRangeUsd }`. `categoryTags` are the Brand's `product_category` values as words (`apparel`, `denim`…). It never gets the Brand's entity ID or official site. The four rules are written on `AiProvider.tailorPieces` for ticket 34's prompt; only "never a price" can be checked in code, and already is. The fake adapter was already building generic Top, Bottom and Shoes items.
- **The card.** It follows the Guided steps prototype: "Whole Outfit about $X–$Y" under the title, and each Brand row's price and "Visit <Brand>" on the right on wide screens, under the text on phones. Prices are whole dollars. A row with no price data shows no price, and a row with no https site no link. `OutfitView.priceRange` and `PieceView.brand` carry them.
- **Checked in the browser.** One Full-Body Consultation on local development, with the fake AI and live Qloo (no-preference, United Kingdom, 35–44), built two Outfits with Primark, ASOS, Topshop, Reebok, Zara and Burberry, at desktop and phone widths, with no console or server errors.
  - **For ticket 32:** the Privacy Policy's list of what Workflow run data holds should name Brands too.
  - **For the team:** of the 150 Brands those three calls returned, 17 had an https `official_site`, 35 http and 98 none, so most rows have no "Visit" link under the https-only rule.
- **Live calls used:** about 35 read-only calls on 2026-10-17: 10 tag lookups, 20 Brand searches and up to 5 in the browser check. About 9,627 were left.
