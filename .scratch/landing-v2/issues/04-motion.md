# 04: The motion layer

**What to build:** One client component that brings the chapters to life for visitors who haven't asked for reduced motion, and leaves the static page alone for those who have.

**Blocked by:** [02 The chapters as plain, readable markup](02-static-chapters.md)

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Spec:** [spec.md](../spec.md) "Design"; `landingpagev2/docs/style_guide.md` sections 8, 12 and 13.

- [x] Lenis on mouse and trackpad, synced to GSAP's ticker; in-page links glide.
- [x] Masked line rises, blur-to-focus entrances, staggered lists, the word fill.
- [x] The ground morphing between paper and ink; chapters that pin and dim as the next slides over.
- [x] Pinned Photo Types with the Figure turntable and hotspots; the Style Icons track; the Brands accordion; the Preview grow, wipe and bloom; the cylinder; the stacked number cards.
- [x] Header hide and show, the chapter indicator, loops that play only on screen, the first-visit loader.
