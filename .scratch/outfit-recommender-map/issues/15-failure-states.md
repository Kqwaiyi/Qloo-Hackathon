# What does the user see when a Consultation step fails?

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 08, 10, 12
Map: [Outfit Recommender](../map.md)

## Question

In the guided-steps flow chosen in "Prototype the Consultation flow", what does the user see, and what can they do next, when a step fails?

- The photo check rejects the photo: wrong Photo Type, no person, or more than one person.
- Qloo returns too few Style Icons or Brands for the user's filters and Budget Tier.
- The image model refuses or fails a Preview.
- The user hits a usage limit: Consultations per day, "see more" presses, or Budget Tier re-runs.
- Whether a failed attempt still counts against those limits, and whether the Consultation is kept in history.
